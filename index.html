NEPH3D Privacy Policy

Neph3D Pin Publisher · Last updated: October 7, 2026

About this app

Neph3D Pin Publisher is a private, single-user tool operated by NEPH3D, a small business selling 3D-printed, hand-painted collectibles on Etsy. It publishes Pins to the operator's own Pinterest account, based on the operator's own Instagram posts, with each Pin linking to the matching listing in the NEPH3D Etsy shop.

The app is not offered to the public. Only the NEPH3D account owner signs in to it. It does not collect data from other Pinterest or Instagram users.

Data the app accesses

The app only accesses data belonging to the NEPH3D account owner, through official APIs and with the owner's explicit authorization.

SourceData accessedWhy
PinterestAccount username, list of boards, Pins created by the appTo choose the target board and publish Pins
InstagramThe owner's own posts: image or video URL, caption, post date, post linkTo use each post as the basis for a Pin
EtsyPublic URLs and titles of the owner's own shop listingsTo set each Pin's destination link
OAuth tokensAccess and refresh tokens issued by Pinterest and InstagramTo call the APIs on the owner's behalf

The app does not access followers, messages, comments, analytics of other accounts, or any personal data of third parties.

How the data is used

The data is used for one purpose only: creating and managing Pins on the owner's Pinterest account.

  • Reading new Instagram posts so the owner can review them
  • Letting the owner match each post to an Etsy listing
  • Publishing the Pin, with the image, a title, a description and the Etsy link, to the chosen board
  • Keeping a record of which posts were already published, to avoid duplicates

The data is never used for advertising, profiling, resale, or training any model.

Storage, security and retention

All data is stored on a private server operated by NEPH3D in France. It is not stored with any third-party cloud service.

  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted and are never shared or logged
  • Access to the app is restricted to the owner, over a private network
  • Post records and listing links are kept as long as the app is in use
  • All stored data is deleted within 30 days after the app is permanently retired, or immediately after access is revoked

Sharing with third parties

No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone. The only data sent outside the app is the Pin content published to Pinterest through the official Pinterest API, at the owner's request.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time:

  • Pinterest: Settings > Security and logins > Apps
  • Instagram: Settings > Apps and websites

Revoking access stops all data collection immediately.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data the app holds. Since the app only processes the owner's own account data, such requests are expected to be rare.
